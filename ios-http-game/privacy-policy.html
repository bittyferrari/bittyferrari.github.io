Game Host Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

In short: Game Host does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything happens on your phone and on your local Wi-Fi network.

What the app does

Game Host ("the app") turns your iPhone into a small web server on your local network. Friends on the same Wi-Fi network (or connected to your phone's Personal Hotspot) open the games in a web browser, and you play in the app itself.

Information we collect

We, the developer, do not collect any information. The app does not send data to the developer or to any third party, and contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics or crash reporting.

Information that stays on your devices

  • App settings (such as the server port and whether the server starts automatically) are saved only on your iPhone.
  • Game data — player names you type in, rooms, seats, moves and drawings — is kept in your phone's memory only while the server is running, and is shared only with the people connected to your game on the same local network. It is cleared when the server stops or the app closes.
  • Browsers that join a game may remember a player name on that device so it does not need to be typed again.
  • If you choose to save or share something (for example a drawing as an image), the file is passed to the iOS share sheet and goes only where you send it.

Local network and Bonjour

The app asks for Local Network permission so that devices on your Wi-Fi can connect to the games hosted on your phone. While the server is on, the app announces itself on your local network using Bonjour, which includes your iPhone's device name. This information is visible only to devices on the same local network and is never sent over the internet by the app.

The connection between players is a plain local-network connection (HTTP). Only host games on networks you trust.

External links

If you tap a link to a website outside the app, it opens in Safari. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Game Host 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

簡單來說：Game Host 不會收集、儲存或分享任何個人資料。沒有帳號、沒有廣告、沒有分析工具、也不追蹤你。所有事情都只在你的手機和你的區域網路（Wi-Fi）裡進行。

App 的用途

Game Host（以下稱「本 App」）會在你的 iPhone 上開一個區域網路的小型網頁伺服器。連上同一個 Wi-Fi（或連上這支手機個人熱點）的朋友用瀏覽器打開遊戲，你則直接在 App 裡一起玩。

我們收集的資料

開發者不會收集任何資料。本 App 不會把資料傳給開發者或任何第三方，也沒有使用任何廣告、分析或當機回報的第三方 SDK。

只留在你裝置上的資料

  • App 設定（例如伺服器連接埠、是否自動開啟伺服器）只存在你的 iPhone 上。
  • 遊戲資料：你輸入的玩家名稱、房間、座位、棋步和畫作，只在伺服器開著的時候暫存在手機記憶體裡，也只會分享給在同一個區域網路、加入你遊戲的人。伺服器關閉或 App 結束後就會清除。
  • 加入遊戲的瀏覽器可能會在該裝置上記住玩家名稱，下次就不用再輸入。
  • 如果你選擇儲存或分享內容（例如把畫作存成圖片），檔案會交給 iOS 的分享選單，只會送到你自己選的地方。

本地網路與 Bonjour

本 App 會要求「本地網路」權限，讓同一個 Wi-Fi 上的裝置能連到你手機上的遊戲。伺服器開著時，本 App 會用 Bonjour 在區域網路上廣播，內容包含你 iPhone 的裝置名稱。這些資訊只有同一個區域網路上的裝置看得到，本 App 不會把它傳到網際網路上。

玩家之間的連線是一般的區域網路連線（HTTP），請只在你信任的網路上開遊戲。

外部連結

如果你點了連到 App 以外網站的連結，會用 Safari 開啟，那些網站有各自的隱私權政策。

兒童

本 App 不會在知情的情況下收集任何人（包括 13 歲以下兒童）的個人資料。

政策更新
