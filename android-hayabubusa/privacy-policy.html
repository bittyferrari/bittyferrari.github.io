Hayabubusa Privacy Policy

Effective date / 生效日期：2026-11-12

English繁體中文
In short: Hayabubusa does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. It has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Everything stays on your device.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Hayabubusa Android app (package com.bittyferrari.hayabubusa, “the app”) handles information. The app is developed by bittyferrari (“we”).

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal or usage information. The app does not request the Internet permission, so it cannot send data off your device. It contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other third-party SDKs, and it does not require you to create an account.

3. Access to your files

The app only reads image folders that you explicitly choose through the Android system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). It does not request broad storage permissions and cannot see any folder you have not selected. Images are only read and displayed; the app does not modify, upload, or delete them. You can revoke access at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

4. Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app keeps the following data locally on your device only:

  • Settings such as zoom level, page gap, sort order, and display options.
  • Reading progress: the folders you have opened and your position in each, used for “Continue reading”.
  • Thumbnail cache: small preview images stored in the app's cache folder to speed up browsing.

This data never leaves your device through the app. You can delete it at any time from Android Settings → Apps → Hayabubusa → Storage (Clear cache / Clear storage), or by uninstalling the app.

5. Android backup

If you have enabled Android's system backup, Android may include the app's settings and reading progress in your device backup (for example, to your Google account). This is handled by Android and Google under their own privacy policies, not by us, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings. The thumbnail cache is not backed up.

6. External links

The app contains an optional “Donate” button that opens a PayPal page in your web browser. The app sends nothing to PayPal; anything you do on that page is governed by PayPal's privacy policy.

7. Sharing of information

Because we collect no data, we do not share, sell, or transfer any data to third parties.

8. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact: bittyferrari@gmail.com

簡單來說：Hayabubusa 不會蒐集、上傳、分享或販售任何個人資料。App 沒有網路權限、不需要帳號、沒有廣告，也沒有任何分析追蹤工具。所有資料都只留在你的裝置上。

1. 關於本政策

本政策說明 Hayabubusa Android App（套件名稱 com.bittyferrari.hayabubusa，以下稱「本 App」）如何處理資訊。本 App 由 bittyferrari（以下稱「我們」）開發。

2. 我們蒐集的資訊

我們不蒐集任何個人資料或使用資料。本 App 不要求網路權限，因此無法將任何資料傳出你的裝置。本 App 不含廣告、數據分析、當機回報或其他第三方 SDK，也不需要註冊帳號。

3. 檔案存取

本 App 只會讀取你透過 Android 系統資料夾選擇器（Storage Access Framework）主動選擇的圖片資料夾，不要求完整的儲存空間權限，也無法看到你沒有選擇的資料夾。圖片僅供讀取與顯示，本 App 不會修改、上傳或刪除它們。你可以隨時清除本 App 的資料或解除安裝來撤銷存取權。

4. 儲存在裝置上的資料

為了正常運作，本 App 只會在你的裝置本機保存以下資料：

  • 設定：例如縮放比例、頁面間距、排序方式與顯示選項。
  • 閱讀進度：你開過的資料夾以及在每個資料夾中的位置，用於「繼續閱讀」功能。
  • 縮圖快取：存放在 App 快取資料夾中的小型預覽圖，用來加快瀏覽速度。

這些資料不會經由本 App 離開你的裝置。你可以隨時在 Android「設定 → 應用程式 → Hayabubusa → 儲存空間」中清除快取或清除儲存空間，或直接解除安裝本 App 來刪除這些資料。

5. Android 備份

如果你開啟了 Android 系統備份，Android 可能會將本 App 的設定與閱讀進度納入裝置備份（例如備份到你的 Google 帳戶）。此備份由 Android 與 Google 依其隱私權政策處理，並非由我們處理；你可以在裝置的備份設定中關閉。縮圖快取不會被備份。

6. 外部連結

本 App 有一個選用的「贊助」按鈕，會在瀏覽器中開啟 PayPal 頁面。本 App 不會傳送任何資料給 PayPal；你在該頁面上的任何操作均適用 PayPal 的隱私權政策。

7. 資訊分享

由於我們不蒐集任何資料，因此不會與任何第三方分享、販售或轉移資料。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 不會在知情的情況下蒐集任何人的資訊，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

9. 政策變更

若本政策有所變更，更新後的版本將公布於本頁面，並更新生效日期。

10. 聯絡方式

如對本政策有任何疑問，請聯絡：bittyferrari@gmail.com
