Privacy Policy

App: Japanese Slideshow for iOS (bundle ID com.bittyferrari.japanese-slideshow)
Last updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app Japanese Slideshow ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

The App collects nothing. It has no account system, no servers of our own, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. All sentences, kana readings and translations are bundled inside the App and work fully offline. Nothing you do in the App is transmitted to us.

2. Information We Collect Directly

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not operate our own servers and we do not upload your content anywhere.

3. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only in the App's own sandbox on your device:

  • Your favorites and study history
  • App settings and preferences (auto-advance interval, speech rate and delay, JLPT level filter, interface language, translation language, font sizes, theme, background photo choice, etc.)

This data is not transmitted to us. It is removed when you delete the App. If you have iCloud Backup or iCloud Keychain enabled on your device, a copy of the App's data may be included in your own iCloud backup under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

4. Photos

You may optionally choose photos from your photo library (or a folder in the Files app) to use as the slideshow background. The App reads those images only to display them on your own device. Photos are never uploaded, copied off the device, analyzed, or shared with anyone. The App requests photo access only at the moment you choose to pick a background, and you can revoke it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

5. Speech (Text-to-Speech)

The App uses Apple's built-in speech synthesis (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to read Japanese sentences and translations aloud. The voices are provided by iOS. Most voices run entirely on-device; some enhanced or personal voices provided by Apple may involve Apple's own processing, which is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by this one. We do not receive any speech data. Speech can be turned off in the App's settings.

6. Advertising and Analytics

This version of the App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party trackers. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is requested or used, and the App does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt. If advertising is ever added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the App Store privacy labels will be updated accordingly.

7. Permissions Used

  • Photo library (optional) — only if you choose your own photos as the slideshow background, as described in Section 4.
  • Background audio — so the slideshow keeps reading aloud when the screen is locked or the App is in the background, and so playback controls appear on the Lock Screen.

The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location, health data, calendar, or files outside the ones you explicitly pick.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we do not have any. No data is shared with third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, no personal information about a child can be collected through the App.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by deleting the App. If you have questions about the rights available to you under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, you may contact us at the address below.

11. Security

Data created by the App is kept inside the App's sandbox on your device, protected by iOS, where other apps cannot read it. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
bittyferrari@gmail.com
