Privacy Policy

App: SlideShow for iPhone and iPad (bundle ID com.bittyferrari.slideshow)
Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app SlideShow ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

The App collects nothing. It has no account system, no servers of our own, no analytics, no advertising SDK, and no tracking. Your photos are read on your device only to display them, and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

2. Information We Collect Directly

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not operate our own servers.

3. Photos and Folders

You choose where the slideshow's photos come from:

  • Photo Library — if you choose this source, the App asks for photo library access and reads your photos (all photos or one album) only to display them on your device.
  • Folders in the Files app — including On My iPhone / iPad, iCloud Drive, USB drives, SD card readers and SMB file servers. The App can only read the folder you explicitly pick, and it keeps a bookmark to that folder so it can be opened again next time.

Photos are never uploaded, copied off the device, analyzed, or shared by the App. If your photos are stored in iCloud Photos, iCloud Drive or on a network server you connected in the Files app, iOS may download them from that service so they can be shown; that transfer happens between your device and the service you chose (for example Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy), and we receive nothing. You can revoke photo access at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only in the App's own sandbox on your device:

  • App settings and preferences (play mode, transitions, sort order, display options, interface language, etc.)
  • The folder or album you last chose, and a scan cache of the file list so large folders open faster
  • Temporary resized images kept in memory while the slideshow plays

This data is not transmitted to us. It is removed when you delete the App. If iCloud Backup is enabled on your device, a copy may be included in your own iCloud backup under Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The App contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK and no third-party trackers. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is requested or used, and the App does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. If this ever changes in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy labels will be updated before that version is released.

6. External Links

The App contains an optional donation link that opens PayPal in your browser. Anything you do on PayPal is governed by PayPal's privacy policy, not by this one. The App itself sends no data when you tap the link.

7. Permissions Used

  • Photo library (optional) — only if you choose the Photo Library as a source, as described in Section 3.
  • Folder access — only for folders you pick yourself in the Files app folder picker.

The App does not request access to your contacts, microphone, camera, location, health data or calendar.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade or share your data, because we do not have any. No data is shared with third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected at all, no personal information about a child can be collected through the App.

10. Your Rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by deleting the App. If you have questions about your rights under laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, you may contact us at the address below.

11. Security

Data created by the App is kept inside the App's sandbox on your device, protected by iOS, where other apps cannot read it. No storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
bittyferrari@gmail.com

隱私權政策

應用程式：SlideShow iPhone／iPad 版（Bundle ID com.bittyferrari.slideshow）
最後更新日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

本隱私權政策說明 SlideShow iOS 應用程式（以下稱「本應用程式」）如何處理與您有關的資訊。使用本應用程式即表示您同意本政策。

1. 摘要

本應用程式不蒐集任何資料。沒有帳號系統、沒有我們自己的伺服器、沒有數據分析、沒有廣告 SDK，也沒有任何追蹤。您的照片只會在您的裝置上讀取並顯示，絕不會上傳給我們或任何人。

2. 我們直接蒐集的資訊

本應用程式不需要註冊帳號，也不會要求您提供姓名、電子郵件、電話號碼或位置等個人資料。我們沒有自行架設的伺服器。

3. 照片與資料夾

幻燈片的照片來源由您自行選擇：

  • 照片圖庫：選擇此來源時，本應用程式會請求相簿權限，並僅為了在您的裝置上顯示而讀取照片（全部照片或指定相簿）。
  • 「檔案」App 中的資料夾：包含我的 iPhone／iPad、iCloud 雲碟、USB 隨身碟、SD 讀卡機與 SMB 檔案伺服器。本應用程式只能讀取您主動選擇的資料夾，並會保存該資料夾的書籤，以便下次直接開啟。

本應用程式絕不會上傳、外傳、分析或分享您的照片。若照片存放在 iCloud 照片、iCloud 雲碟，或您在「檔案」App 中連線的網路伺服器上，iOS 可能會從該服務下載照片以供顯示；這是您的裝置與您所選服務之間的傳輸（例如 Apple，適用 Apple 的隱私權政策），我們不會取得任何資料。您可隨時於 設定 › 隱私權與安全性 › 照片 中撤回相簿權限。

4. 儲存在您裝置上的資料

下列資料僅儲存在您裝置上本應用程式的沙箱（sandbox）中：

  • 應用程式設定與偏好（播放模式、轉場、排序方式、顯示選項、介面語言等）
  • 您上次選擇的資料夾或相簿，以及讓大型資料夾能更快開啟的檔案清單掃描快取
  • 播放時暫存在記憶體中的縮小版影像

這些資料不會傳送給我們，刪除 App 時即會一併移除。若您的裝置啟用了 iCloud 備份，這些資料可能包含在您自己的 iCloud 備份中，並適用 Apple 的條款；我們無法存取。

5. 廣告與數據分析

本應用程式不含任何廣告 SDK、數據分析 SDK 或第三方追蹤器，不會請求或使用廣告識別碼（IDFA），也不會顯示 App 追蹤透明度（ATT）詢問視窗。若未來版本有所變更，我們會在該版本上架前更新本政策與 App Store 隱私標籤。

6. 外部連結

本應用程式提供一個選用的贊助連結，點選後會在瀏覽器中開啟 PayPal。您在 PayPal 上的任何操作適用 PayPal 的隱私權政策，而非本政策。點選連結時，本應用程式本身不會傳送任何資料。

7. 使用的權限

  • 相簿（選用）：僅在您選擇照片圖庫作為來源時使用，如第 3 節所述。
  • 資料夾存取：僅限您在「檔案」資料夾選擇器中自行選擇的資料夾。

本應用程式不會要求存取您的通訊錄、麥克風、相機、位置、健康資料或行事曆。

8. 資料分享

我們不會販售、出租、交換或分享您的資料，因為我們並未持有任何資料。本應用程式不會與第三方、資料仲介或廣告聯播網分享任何資料。

9. 兒童

本應用程式並非以未滿 13 歲兒童為對象，且不會在知情的情況下蒐集任何人的個人資料，兒童亦然。由於本應用程式完全不蒐集資料，因此不可能透過本應用程式蒐集到兒童的個人資訊。

10. 您的權利

由於我們並未持有您的個人資料，因此沒有可供我們代為存取、更正、匯出或刪除的內容。儲存於您裝置上的資料，您可隨時透過刪除 App 來清除。若您對 GDPR、CCPA 等法規賦予您的權利有任何疑問，歡迎透過下方聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

11. 安全性
