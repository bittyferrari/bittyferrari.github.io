隱私權政策 · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

In short: HeatCheck does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal data. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. Everything it reads stays on your device.

1. Who we are

HeatCheck (package name com.bittyferrari.heatcheck) is an Android app developed by bittyferrari (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Information the app reads

To show temperatures and device details, HeatCheck reads the following information locally on your device:

  • CPU, GPU and other thermal sensor readings (from system files such as /sys/class/thermal);
  • Battery temperature, voltage, current and health;
  • Memory and storage usage, CPU core frequencies;
  • Device information such as model, manufacturer, Android version, security patch level, kernel version, SoC, GPU name and display specs.

This information is displayed in the app, widget, picture-in-picture window, floating overlay and notification. It is never transmitted off your device. The app does not request the Internet permission and therefore cannot send data anywhere.

HeatCheck does not access your contacts, photos, files, location, camera, microphone, phone number, advertising ID or any other personal identifiers.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves only your preferences (for example language, picture-in-picture ratio, font size, overlay opacity and layout) in the app’s private storage on your device. These settings contain no personal information. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the system may include these settings in your own Google device backup, which is governed by Google’s privacy policy. Uninstalling the app removes them.

5. Clipboard

The “Copy all” button in Advanced Info copies device details to your clipboard only when you tap it. The app does not read your clipboard.

6. Third-party services and links

HeatCheck contains no third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, tracking or crash reporting. The app may include an optional donation link (PayPal). Tapping it opens your web browser; any information you provide there is handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy, not by us.

7. Data sharing and sale

We do not collect any data, so we do not share or sell data with anyone.

8. Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

9. Security

Because no data leaves your device and the app has no network access, there is no data in transit or on servers to protect. Preferences are kept in app-private storage protected by Android’s app sandbox.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes (for example, if a future version starts using the internet) will be reflected here before release.

HeatCheck（套件名稱 com.bittyferrari.heatcheck）是由 bittyferrari（以下稱「我們」）開發的 Android App。本政策說明 App 如何處理資訊。

2. App 讀取的資訊

為了顯示溫度與裝置資訊，HeatCheck 會在你的裝置本機讀取以下資訊：

  • CPU、GPU 及其他溫度感測器數值（來自 /sys/class/thermal 等系統檔案）；
  • 電池溫度、電壓、電流與健康度；
  • 記憶體與儲存空間使用量、CPU 各核心頻率；
  • 裝置資訊，例如型號、製造商、Android 版本、安全性修補程式等級、核心版本、SoC、GPU 名稱與螢幕規格。

這些資訊只會顯示在 App、桌面 Widget、子母畫面、懸浮視窗與通知中，絕不會傳送到裝置以外。App 沒有申請網路權限，因此無法將資料傳送到任何地方。

HeatCheck 不會存取你的聯絡人、相片、檔案、位置、相機、麥克風、電話號碼、廣告 ID 或任何其他個人識別資訊。

3. 儲存在裝置上的資訊

App 只會把你的偏好設定（例如語言、子母畫面比例、字體大小、懸浮視窗不透明度與排列方式）存在裝置上的 App 私有儲存空間，這些設定不含任何個人資訊。若你的裝置開啟了 Android 備份，系統可能會把這些設定包含在你自己的 Google 裝置備份中，該備份適用 Google 的隱私權政策。解除安裝 App 即會刪除這些設定。
