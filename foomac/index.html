FAQ

常見問題Questions

真的免費嗎？Is it really free?

是的，沒有試用期、沒有廣告，也不會收集任何資料。Yes — no trial, no ads, and no data collection.

會動到我的音樂檔嗎？Does it change my files?

不會。Foomac 只讀取，不會搬移、改名或改標籤；zip 也不會被解壓到你的資料夾，播放時只解一首到暫存檔。No. Foomac only reads — it never moves, renames or retags anything, and zips are never extracted into your folders; one song at a time goes to a temporary file.

支援哪些格式？Which formats work?

mp3、m4a、aac、flac、wav、aiff，以及裝著這些檔案的 zip。加密的 zip 目前不支援。mp3, m4a, aac, flac, wav and aiff — plus zips containing them. Encrypted zips aren’t supported yet.

音樂庫存在哪裡？Where is the library stored?

掃描快取在 ~/Library/Application Support/Foomac/library.json，刪掉它只會讓下次掃描慢一點。The scan cache lives in ~/Library/Application Support/Foomac/library.json. Deleting it only makes the next scan slower.

支援哪些 Mac？Which Macs are supported?

macOS 14 Sonoma 以上，Apple silicon 和 Intel 都可以。macOS 14 Sonoma or later, on Apple silicon and Intel.

發現問題或有建議？Found a bug or have an idea?
