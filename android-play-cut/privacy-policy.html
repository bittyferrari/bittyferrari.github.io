Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the PlayCut application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The App does not gather your name, email address, location, contacts, advertising ID, or usage analytics, and we operate no servers or accounts of our own. The App contains no advertising.

2. Your Videos and Files

The App plays, cuts, crops, and exports videos entirely on your device. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere.

  • Opening videos and subtitles: the App can only read the files and folders you choose yourself through the Android system file picker. If you add a folder, the App keeps read access to that folder so you can browse it later. You can remove the folder in the App at any time, which also releases that access.
  • Exports and screenshots: exported clips are saved to Movies/PlayCut and screenshots to Pictures/PlayCut on your device.
  • Data stored on your device: the App stores your flags (cut points), your list of added folders, and your settings (such as theme, language, and subtitle size) locally on your device. This data is used only by the App to work properly, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Permissions

The App does not request access to your photos, camera, microphone, location, or contacts. Access to your videos is granted only through the files and folders you pick yourself, as described above.

The App declares network permissions (Internet and network state) for Google Play services such as in-app purchases. It does not use them to send your data, videos, or usage information to us.

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

  • Google Play: if the App offers an optional in-app purchase, payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Donations: if you choose to tap the optional donation button, your web browser or the PayPal app will open an external PayPal page. Anything you do on that page is handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy, not by the App.
  • Android backup: if you have Android device backup turned on, the system may include the App's settings in your own Google account backup. This is controlled by Android and your device settings, not by us.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves, so there is no user data held by us that could be exposed. All video processing runs locally on your device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:

bittyferrari@gmail.com
