Privacy Policy

App: Mochabar — a Windows-style taskbar for macOS
Bundle IDs: com.bittyferrari.mochabar (Mac App Store), com.bitty.TaskbarForMac (website & Steam)
Effective date: October 9, 2026 · Last updated: October 9, 2026

Short version: Mochabar does not collect, store, or share any personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking. Everything it reads to show you your taskbar, calendar, or system status stays on your Mac. The Mac App Store version never connects to the internet at all.

1. What Mochabar does

Mochabar adds a Windows-style taskbar, Start menu, clock and calendar, system tray, animated scenes, and mini-games to macOS. All of this runs locally on your Mac.

2. Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of your personal data, including names, email addresses, device identifiers, location, contacts, calendar events, keystrokes, audio, usage analytics, or crash reports. Mochabar contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or tracking.

3. Permissions and how they are used

Some features need macOS permissions. Each is optional, requested only when you use the related feature, and processed entirely on your Mac:

PermissionUsed forData leaves your Mac?
Input MonitoringDetecting a single tap of the ⌘ key to open the Start menu. It is a listen-only check for that one key; no keystrokes are recorded or stored.No
CalendarsShowing your events in the calendar that pops up from the clock.No
Screen & System Audio RecordingThe optional "react to music" lighting effect, which computes sound levels (a frequency spectrum) from system audio in real time. Nothing is recorded, saved, or captured from the screen.No
BluetoothShowing whether Bluetooth is on in the system tray.No
Accessibility, Automation (website & Steam versions only)Listing and switching individual windows, and running Lock / Log Out / Restart / Shut Down from the Start menu.No

Mochabar also reads public system information such as running apps, battery level, volume, network status, CPU and memory usage, and installed applications, only to display them on the taskbar.

4. Data stored on your Mac

Your settings, pinned apps, game progress and scores, and any custom Start-button image are stored only in the app's local storage on your Mac (for the App Store version: ~/Library/Containers/com.bittyferrari.mochabar/). The developer cannot read them. If you use "Export settings", the file is saved wherever you choose and is shared only if you share it yourself. Deleting the app and its data removes everything.

5. Network connections

  • Mac App Store version: makes no network connections. Purchases are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Steam version: makes no network connections of its own. Purchases are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.
  • Website version: only when you activate, verify, or deactivate a Pro license, the app sends your license key and your Mac's computer name (so you can tell your devices apart when managing activations) to Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider, at api.lemonsqueezy.com. Purchases are handled under the Lemon Squeezy Privacy Policy. No other data is sent.

6. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties. Apart from the license check described above for the website version, Mochabar does not communicate with any server.

7. Children's privacy

Mochabar is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

8. Your rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no data held by us to access, correct, or delete. You can revoke any permission at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security. For license records kept by Lemon Squeezy, you can manage your orders at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders or contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the release notes.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: bittyferrari@gmail.com
