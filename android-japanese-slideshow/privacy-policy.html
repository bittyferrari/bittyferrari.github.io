Privacy Policy

App: Japanese Slideshow (package com.bitty.jaslideshow)
Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the app Japanese Slideshow ("the App", "we") handles information when you use it. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect Directly

The App does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. We do not operate our own servers and we do not upload your content anywhere.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is created and stored only in the App's private storage on your device:

  • Learning content, decks and phrase lists included with or imported into the App
  • Playback and study progress, favorites and history
  • App settings and preferences (playback speed, speech settings, interface language, theme, etc.)

This data is not transmitted to us. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. If Android Auto Backup is enabled on your device, a copy of this data may be backed up to your own Google account under Google's terms; we have no access to it.

3. Advertising

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / App Set ID)
  • Approximate (coarse, IP-based) location and country
  • Device information such as device model, operating system version and language
  • App usage and ad interaction events (ad impressions, clicks) for reporting and fraud prevention

This collection is performed by Google, not by us. We do not receive your personal data from these partners — only aggregated, anonymous performance reports.

Google's use of data is described in Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy, and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message (Google UMP / IAB TCF) before personalized ads are used, and may change or withdraw that consent at any time from within the App. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's Android settings under Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Text-to-Speech

The App uses the text-to-speech (TTS) engine installed on your device to read Japanese text aloud. If the TTS engine you selected is a cloud-based engine, the text to be spoken may be sent to that engine's provider for processing. That processing is governed by the privacy policy of the TTS engine provider (for example Google or Samsung), not by us. You can change or disable the TTS engine in your device settings.

5. Permissions

  • Foreground service / media playback — to keep the slideshow and audio playing while the App is in the background and to show playback controls in the notification area.
  • Notifications — to display the playback control notification.

The App does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location, or files outside its own storage.

6. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the advertising services in the App, please contact us and we will help you request its deletion from the relevant provider.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data. The only third party that processes data in connection with the App is Google (AdMob), as described in Section 3, and — depending on your device configuration — your TTS engine provider, as described in Section 4.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, requests to access, correct or delete data should be directed to Google for advertising data (see Google's privacy policy above). Data stored on your device can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

9. Security

Data created by the App is kept in the App's private storage area, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact:
bittyferrari@gmail.com
