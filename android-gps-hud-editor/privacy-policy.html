繁體中文

摘要：GPS HUD 不會收集、上傳或分享任何個人資料。所有影片、GPX 軌跡與 HUD 的處理與輸出，全部在您的裝置上本機完成。本應用程式不含分析工具、廣告 SDK 或第三方追蹤。

1. 本應用程式做什麼

GPS HUD 讓您匯入自己的影片與 GPX 軌跡檔，在影片上疊加速度、海拔、坡度等 HUD 資訊，預覽後輸出成新的 MP4 檔案。整個流程皆在裝置本機以 Android Media3 完成。

2. 我們處理哪些資料

資料類型用途是否離開您的裝置
您選擇的影片檔預覽與輸出加上 HUD 的影片否，僅在本機處理
您選擇的 GPX 檔（含位置座標、時間、海拔）計算速度、距離、爬升、坡度並繪製 HUD否，僅在本機處理
應用程式設定（HUD 樣式、單位、時間偏移等）記住您的偏好設定否，儲存在應用程式的本機空間

3. 我們不做的事

  • 不收集姓名、電子郵件、電話、帳號或任何可識別身分的資料。
  • 不向伺服器上傳您的影片、GPX 檔或位置資料。我們不經營任何後端伺服器。
  • 不使用分析、統計、當機回報或廣告 SDK。
  • 不將任何資料販售、出租或分享給第三方。
  • 不在背景或使用中即時存取裝置的 GPS／定位感測器。位置資訊僅來自您自行選擇匯入的 GPX 檔。

4. 權限說明

權限為什麼需要
存取照片與影片（由您透過系統檔案選擇器授權）讀取您指定的影片與 GPX 檔，並將輸出的 MP4 寫入您的相簿。應用程式僅能存取您主動選取的檔案。
POST_NOTIFICATIONS在影片輸出進行中與完成時顯示進度通知。
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE由 Android 系統元件宣告；本應用程式的功能不需連線，也不會傳送您的任何資料。

5. 資料保留與刪除

本應用程式不在任何伺服器保存資料。設定檔與快取儲存在應用程式的私有空間；您可在「設定 → 應用程式 → GPS HUD → 儲存空間」清除資料，或直接卸載應用程式，即可完全移除。您輸出的影片屬於您自己的檔案，可隨時在相簿中刪除。

6. 兒童隱私

本應用程式不針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，也不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。由於本應用程式完全不收集個人資料，亦不存在此類風險。

7. 政策變更

若本政策有所更新，我們會修改本頁內容並更新上方的「最後更新」日期。若未來版本新增任何會傳送資料的功能，我們會在該功能推出前先行更新本政策。

8. 聯絡方式

English

Summary: GPS HUD does not collect, upload, or share any personal data. All video, GPX, and HUD processing happens locally on your device. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party tracking.

1. What the app does

GPS HUD lets you import your own video and GPX track files, overlay HUD telemetry (speed, elevation, gradient, distance) onto the video, preview the result, and export a new MP4. All of this runs on your device using Android Media3.

2. Data the app handles

DataPurposeLeaves your device?
Video files you selectPreview and export the HUD-overlaid videoNo — processed locally only
GPX files you select (coordinates, timestamps, elevation)Compute speed, distance, elevation gain and gradient for the HUDNo — processed locally only
App settings (HUD style, units, time offset, etc.)Remember your preferencesNo — stored in the app's local storage

3. What the app does not do

  • No collection of names, email addresses, phone numbers, accounts, or any personally identifiable information.
  • No uploading of your videos, GPX files, or location data. We operate no backend servers.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of any data with third parties.
  • No access to your device's live GPS or location sensors, in the foreground or background. Location data comes only from GPX files you choose to import.

4. Permissions

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photos and video access (granted by you through the system file picker)To read the video and GPX files you select, and to write the exported MP4 into your gallery. The app can only reach files you explicitly pick.
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo show export progress and completion notifications.
INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEDeclared by Android system components. The app's features require no connectivity and transmit none of your data.

5. Data retention and deletion

No data is retained on any server. Settings and caches live in the app's private storage. You can remove them via Settings → Apps → GPS HUD → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Exported videos are your own files and can be deleted from your gallery at any time.

6. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Since the app collects no personal data at all, no such data can be gathered.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated along with the "Last updated" date above. Should a future version introduce any feature that transmits data, this policy will be updated before that feature ships.

8. Contact

Questions about this privacy policy: bittyferrari@gmail.com
