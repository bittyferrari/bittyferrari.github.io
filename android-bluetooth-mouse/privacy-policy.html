Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Bluetooth Mouse application ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We, the developer, do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information ourselves. The App does not gather your name, email address, location, contacts, or usage analytics, and we operate no servers or accounts of our own.

The App does, however, display advertising supplied by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect certain information automatically from your device, as described in section 4 below. We have no access to that information in a form that identifies you individually.

2. Bluetooth Usage

The App uses your device's Bluetooth solely to connect to and control another device as a mouse/pointer. All Bluetooth pairing and communication happens locally between your devices. No data from these connections is recorded, stored, or sent to us or any third party.

3. Permissions

The App may request Bluetooth-related permissions (such as Bluetooth Connect and, where required by the operating system, Nearby Devices or Location) only to discover and connect to Bluetooth devices. These permissions are used exclusively for the App's core functionality and never for tracking or data collection.

The App also requests Internet and network-state permissions, together with the advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID), solely so that the advertising described in section 4 can be delivered.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App displays a banner advertisement at the bottom of its settings screen. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. AdMob is the only third-party service integrated into the App; we use no other analytics or tracking SDKs.

To select and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and interactions with the ads shown. This processing is carried out by Google as an independent party under its own privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can limit ad personalisation at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads, where you may opt out of personalised advertising or delete/reset your advertising ID.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent message may be shown before personalised ads are served, in accordance with applicable law. You may change or withdraw your consent at any time through that message or through your device's ad settings.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Because the App contains advertising, we do not designate it as a child-directed app. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Data Security

We do not collect or store any personal data ourselves, so there is no user data held by us that could be exposed. All mouse and Bluetooth operations run locally on your device. Information processed for advertising purposes is handled by Google under its own security practices.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
