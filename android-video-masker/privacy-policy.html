簡單來說：本 App 只在您的手機上處理您自己選擇的影片，不會收集、上傳、分享任何個人資料或影片內容，也沒有廣告和分析工具。

1. 我們不收集任何資料

影片遮蔽（以下稱「本 App」）不會收集、儲存、傳送或分享任何個人資料，包括但不限於姓名、電子郵件、位置、裝置識別碼、聯絡人、使用紀錄或影片內容。本 App 不需要建立帳號，也不需要登入。

2. 影片如何處理

  • 您透過系統的「相片挑選器」或從相簿「分享」選擇影片，本 App 只能存取您選擇的那些影片。
  • 人臉與車牌偵測使用內建於 App 中的模型，全部在您的裝置上運算，影片畫面與偵測結果不會傳送到任何伺服器。
  • 處理完成的影片會儲存在您裝置相簿的「影片／Movies/VideoMasker」資料夾，只有您可以存取、刪除或分享。
  • 本 App 不會修改或刪除您的原始影片。

3. 權限

本 App 不需要任何特殊權限，不會要求存取相機、麥克風、位置、聯絡人或整個儲存空間，也沒有申請網路存取權限。

4. 第三方服務

本 App 不包含任何廣告、分析、當機回報或追蹤工具，也不會與任何第三方分享資料。若您透過 Google Play 下載本 App，Google 可能會依其自身的隱私權政策處理下載與安裝相關資訊，這部分不在本 App 的控制範圍內。

5. 本機設定

本 App 可能會在您的裝置上記住介面語言、畫質等偏好設定。這些設定只存在於您的裝置上，不會被傳送出去；解除安裝本 App 時會一併移除。

6. 兒童隱私

本 App 不會收集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下兒童。

7. 政策變更

若本政策有所修改，我們會更新本頁面並修改上方的生效日期。若日後新增任何會影響資料處理方式的功能，會在本頁面清楚說明。

8. 聯絡我們

如對本隱私權政策有任何疑問，請寄信至：bittyferrari@gmail.com

In short: This app processes only the videos you choose, entirely on your device. It does not collect, upload or share any personal data or video content, and it contains no ads or analytics.

1. We do not collect any data

Video Masker (“the App”) does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data, including but not limited to names, email addresses, location, device identifiers, contacts, usage data or video content. The App does not require an account or sign-in.

2. How your videos are processed

  • You select videos through the system Photo Picker or by sharing them from your gallery. The App can access only the videos you select.
  • Face and license-plate detection uses models bundled with the App and runs entirely on your device. Video frames and detection results are never sent to any server.
  • Processed videos are saved to your device gallery under “Movies/VideoMasker”, where only you can access, delete or share them.
  • The App does not modify or delete your original videos.

3. Permissions

The App requests no special permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or full device storage, and it does not request network access.

4. Third-party services

The App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools and shares no data with third parties. If you download the App from Google Play, Google may process download and installation information under its own Privacy Policy, which is outside the App’s control.

5. Local settings

The App may remember preferences such as interface language and output quality on your device. These settings stay on your device, are never transmitted, and are removed when you uninstall the App.

6. Children’s privacy

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a future feature changes how data is handled, it will be clearly described here.

8. Contact
