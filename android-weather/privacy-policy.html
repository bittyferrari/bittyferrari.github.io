Overview

YoWeather (the "App", package name com.bittyferrari.yoweather) is developed by independent developer Bitty Ferrari. The App requires no account, contains no ads, and uses no analytics or tracking tools. The developer does not operate any server that collects your data.

Data the App uses

Location permission is optional. You can deny it and search for cities manually; the App still works. You can revoke it anytime in Settings > Apps > YoWeather > Permissions. The App only gets your location while it is open or when its widget refreshes, and does not track you in the background.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com): weather forecasts, air quality and location search. Requests contain coordinates or search terms plus standard connection information such as your IP address. See the Open-Meteo terms and privacy notice.
  • Android system Geocoder: converts coordinates into place names, handled by your device's system services (provided by Google on most devices).
  • Android Auto Backup: if backup is enabled on your device, your saved places and preferences are encrypted and backed up to your Google account so they can be restored on a new phone. This is managed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • PayPal: the in-app "Support" link opens PayPal in your browser; the App never handles payment information.

Sharing and sale of data

The developer does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data with anyone.

Data deletion

All data lives on your device. Uninstalling the App, or using "Clear storage" in system settings, deletes everything. Backups in your Google account can be managed under Backups in Google Drive.

Security

All connections between the App and Open-Meteo are encrypted with HTTPS.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
