隱私權政策 — SlideShow

Effective date / 生效日期: August 13, 2026
Last updated / 最後更新: August 13, 2026
App / 應用程式: SlideShow (com.bittyferrari.slideshow)
Developer / 開發者: Bitty Ferrari

Short version: SlideShow collects nothing. It has no internet permission, no analytics, no ads, and no accounts. Everything stays on your device.
簡短版本:SlideShow 不收集任何資料。沒有網路權限、 沒有分析工具、沒有廣告、也不需要帳號,所有資料都留在你的裝置上。

1. Information We Collect / 我們收集的資料

None. SlideShow does not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data. We do not collect names, email addresses, contacts, location, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, crash reports, or usage analytics.

沒有。SlideShow 不會收集、上傳、販售或分享任何個人或敏感資料, 包含姓名、電子郵件、聯絡人、位置、廣告識別碼、裝置識別碼、當機報告或使用分析。

2. Photos and Media Access / 相片與媒體存取

SlideShow displays images from folders that you explicitly choose using the Android system folder picker (Storage Access Framework). The app requests no broad storage permissions such as READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE.

  • Your images are read only to decode and display them on screen.
  • They are never uploaded, copied off the device, modified, analyzed, or shared.
  • Access is limited to the specific folder you picked (including USB OTG and SD card), and Android keeps that grant so you don't have to re-pick it on every launch.
  • You can revoke access at any time by choosing a different folder, or by clearing the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → SlideShow → Storage.

App 只會讀取你透過系統資料夾選取器(SAF)明確指定的資料夾,不要求任何完整儲存權限。 圖片僅用於在畫面上顯示,不會被上傳、複製、修改或分享。你隨時可以更換資料夾,或到 設定 → 應用程式 → SlideShow → 儲存空間 清除資料以撤銷授權。

3. Data Stored on Your Device / 儲存於裝置上的資料

SlideShow saves only your own preferences locally (selected folder URI, slideshow interval, transition effect, sort order, shuffle/loop settings, scale mode, last viewed image, and language choice), plus a temporary image cache used to make playback smooth. This data never leaves your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

App 只會在本機儲存你的設定(資料夾位置、播放間隔、轉場、排序、隨機/循環、縮放模式、 上次觀看位置、語言)以及暫存的圖片快取。這些資料不會離開裝置,解除安裝或清除資料後即消失。

4. Internet and Network Use / 網路使用

The app does not declare the INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending any data off your device.

App 未宣告 INTERNET 權限,因此在技術上無法將任何資料傳出裝置。

5. Third-Party Services / 第三方服務

SlideShow contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. No data is shared with any third party. Distribution through Google Play is subject to Google's own privacy policy, which is outside our control.

App 不包含任何第三方分析、廣告、追蹤或當機回報 SDK,也不會與任何第三方分享資料。 透過 Google Play 下載安裝時,適用 Google 自身的隱私權政策。

6. Children's Privacy / 兒童隱私

SlideShow does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), because it does not collect data at all.

由於 App 完全不收集資料,自然也不會收集兒童(未滿 13 歲或當地法定年齡)的資料。

7. Data Deletion / 資料刪除

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To remove everything stored locally, clear the app's data or uninstall SlideShow.

我們手上沒有你的任何資料。若要刪除本機資料,請清除 App 資料或解除安裝。

8. Your Rights / 你的權利

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights of access, correction, and deletion over personal data held about you. Since SlideShow processes no personal data and we hold none, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. You may still contact us with any question.

GDPR、CCPA 等法規賦予你查閱、更正與刪除個資的權利;因本 App 不處理任何個資, 故無資料可供查閱或刪除。如有疑問仍歡迎與我們聯絡。

9. Changes to This Policy / 政策變更

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of the app after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

政策若有變更,將於本網址更新並修改生效日期。
